# Brand core

Step two of the rebuild. This page does not depend on the name; the identity (logo, colour, type,
motion) and the design are built on it. It replaces the positioning parts of `BRAND.md`, which was
written for the earlier "Slate" identity.

## Who it is for

Someone who follows several competitive scenes at once (Counter-Strike, League of Legends,
Trackmania, chess, Rocket League; football and other sports later) and is tired of piecing the
picture together from wikis, league sites, social feeds and streams.

They open it with one of four questions:

1. What is on now?
2. What is next, and when, in my time?
3. How is my team or player doing?
4. Where does this tournament stand?

## Promise

**Everything you follow, in one clear view: what is on, what is next, where it stands.**

Short form: **Know what's on, without looking it up.**

## Character

- **Clear.** One glance answers the question. Plain words, exact times in your own time zone.
- **Solid.** Facts you can rely on, with their source. A layout that holds still. Nothing flimsy.
- **Alive.** Live sport is the one thing allowed to move and glow, and big moments get a big stage.

Calm by default, spectacular on purpose.

## What it is not

- Not a wiki: no walls of tables, nothing to edit.
- Not a news site: no articles, no opinions.
- Not a betting product: no odds, ever.
- Not a social network: no feed, no comments.
- Not a stats lab: depth serves following, not analysis.

## Where it sits

My reading of the field, to be checked as each source is integrated:

| Product                      | Strength               | Gap this fills                             |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Liquipedia                   | Complete               | Dense; built to document, not to glance at |
| HLTV, lolesports, Lichess    | Deep on one game       | One scene each                             |
| Sofascore, Flashscore        | Every sport            | Noisy; betting throughout                  |
| Apple Sports                 | Care and calm          | No esports; little depth                   |
| Juked (closed November 2022) | "TV guide for esports" | Gave up the guide to become a social app   |

Position: **the care of the best native apps, the completeness of a wiki, for the scenes you follow.**

## Design principles

Taken from Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and from the standard of the EA FC player pages.

1. **Content is the colour.** Teams, games and tournaments bring their colours; the brand stays
   neutral so they can.
2. **One signal.** Live is the thing that is lit and moving. No colour is reserved for it, and
   nothing else moves on its own.
3. **One moment per screen.** Each screen gives a single thing the big treatment; the rest is quiet.
4. **Glance first, depth on demand.** The answer is visible before any tap; detail is one step away.
5. **Exact and sourced.** Times are local and precise. Where a number comes from is always shown.
6. **Motion means something.** It marks live, start and change. It is brief and can be turned off.

## Voice

Plain, exact, calm, honest. "T1 are out. Nothing else is scheduled for them this year." beats
"Heartbreak for T1 fans".

## The text

> Bearing is a small web app for following esports schedules and tournaments across a few games
> (Counter-Strike 2, League of Legends, Rocket League, Trackmania, and chess). It answers three
> questions for someone who follows a handful of teams and players: what is on now, what is next
> for the things I follow, and where does an event stand. It is built by one person, me, as a
> personal project. It is for viewers; it has nothing to do with betting, odds or fantasy games.
>
> What I would like to use from LiquipediaDB, read on a schedule by a server and never by the
> visitor's browser:
>
> - Trackmania: tournaments, stages, matches and results. I have found no other source at all
>   for Trackmania, so this is the main reason for asking.
> - For Counter-Strike 2 and Rocket League: map and game scores of finished matches.
> - Placements and prize money by place for the events shown.
> - Transfers, to say since when a player has been in a team.
>
> How it is used: the server asks at most once an hour for the schedule of events that are
> running or about to start, and keeps the answers in its own store, so the number of requests
> does not grow with the number of visitors. I will stay inside whatever rate limit you set and
> can lower the frequency further. Every page that shows a fact from Liquipedia names Liquipedia
> as the source, with a link, under CC-BY-SA 3.0.
>
> Bearing is not a copy of Liquipedia and does not try to be one. It shows no wiki pages, no
> articles and no history beyond the last sixty days; it draws a schedule and the state of a
> match or bracket, and points to the source for anything deeper.
>
> Where it will be published: as a public website at https://bearing.vincentmay.com, and the
> code as open source (AGPL-3.0) at https://github.com/vincentmay/bearing.
>
> How it is monetised: it is not. There are no advertisements, no subscriptions, no affiliate
> links and no tracking. Should that ever change, I would ask you for a commercial plan before
> the change, not after.

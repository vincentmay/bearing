Where a team's colour may appear

As lightBehind a live match: a soft pool at each end, never a flat fill.
As a dotOne thing won. Flat, with a hairline edge so pale colours hold on white.
As a discBoth teams of a match that has not started. The ring around it is the wait.

Never as text, as a button, or as the page background. Words and controls stay ink, so a page is readable whoever is playing.

When two teams look alike

If the two colours are too close to tell apart, the second-named team switches to its second colour. If it has none, it takes ink.

When there is no colour

Players without colours, such as chess players, get ink and white. Races with many drivers get a fixed set of four, in starting order.

Live · round 17

An ink dot with a ring leaving it every 1.8 seconds. It is the only looping motion a list is allowed, and it appears nowhere except on something live.

14 seconds, there and back

The two pools of team colour move a tenth of their width, slowly enough that you notice it only when you look for it.

Next steps forward

When a match ends, it drops away and the next one comes to the front, in half a second, easing out. Position is what says "this is the one to watch".

For people who turn motion off: every loop stops, and state changes happen at once. Nothing depends on motion to be understood: a live match is still the lit card at the front with an open dot.

The idea

Everything is a line with stops. Behind you is solid black, ahead is pale, and the stop you are at is red.

Why it could work

People already read line maps without thinking. A day, a best-of-three and a team's path to the final all draw the same way.

The risk

It is the plainest route. It lives on perfect type and spacing, and it has no spectacle until the line moves.

The idea

You ask what is on and it tells you, in words. One sentence for now, a contents page for later. The logo is just the name with a full stop.

Why it could work

Nothing else in esports looks like this. It reads as trustworthy and grown-up, and a sentence works the same for chess, Trackmania or football.

The risk

The sentences have to be written well for every game. With ten matches live at once, reading is slower than scanning.

The idea

The brand owns no colour. Each match is two flat halves in the teams' colours with the ball between them, and the logo does the same.

Why it could work

You recognise a match by its colours before you read a word. It is the loudest route and still very simple, and it carries straight over to football.

The risk

Two teams in similar colours, or colours that clash badly. A long list gets loud. Solo events only have one colour to show.

The idea

Time has depth. The live match is the card in front of you; the next ones stand behind it, smaller and softer the later they are. Scrolling pulls the evening towards you.

Why it could work

It needs no colour at all to say what matters, so team colours stay free. It is the route that would use real 3D best, and Three.js is already in the stack.

The risk

It shows fewer matches at once than a plain list, and the motion has to be very good or it feels like a gimmick.

The idea

The two teams light the whole screen from behind, and you read the match through glass. The score sits in a round lens: the ball of the bearing.

New since C

The lens replaces the plain glass box, the light now moves slowly, and the live match colours the whole home screen, not just its own card.

The risk

It is the closest to Apple's own apps, so the least its own. Blur is heavy on older phones, and text over colour needs care to stay readable.

The evening stands in a queue in front of you. Each card glows faintly in its teams' colours and gets brighter and sharper as it comes forward.

Archivo in sentence case, calmer than the capitals of route 4. Matte colour, real shadows, one steel ball.

The idea

You read who is ahead from across the room: the leading team's colour takes more of the plate, and the ball sits on the seam with the score.

Built from

The colour halves of 4, the stacking and shadows of 5, the ball of every route.

How live is shown

Movement. A live seam is off-centre and the ball rolls on it. A match that has not started rests at exactly half and half.

The risk

It is the loudest. Two teams in similar colours need a rule, and a full day is a lot of colour on one screen.

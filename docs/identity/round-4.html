MY PICK

bearing

The ring of rounds with one dot on it: the round being played. The one from route 7, and the boldest at small sizes.

B · Rings

bearing

A real bearing seen from the side, the one you called not too bad. Drawn heavier here. It thins out most at 16 pixels.

C · Stem

bearing

A letter b made of a line and a ball. The simplest and the most like a letter; it says the least about scores.

D · Dot ring

bearing

The scoreboard itself: eight rounds, one still open. It carries two team colours best, but it can be mistaken for a loading spinner and nearly disappears at 16 pixels.

E · Dotted b

bearing

A letter b whose stem is a row of rounds. It joins the name and the dot rule in one sign. It is the most unusual and the busiest.

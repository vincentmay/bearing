2Logo A, made its own You are right that an open ring with a dot is a common shape. Three ways to make it specific to a bearing, next to the original.

A · As it was

bearing

A ring that stops short, with a small dot in the gap. Clean, but the same idea as many progress, power and loading icons.

MY PICK

A2 · Seated

bearing

The ball is larger than the ring is thick, and the ring is cut to hold it with an even gap all round. That is how a ball sits in a real bearing, and it is the detail other ring logos do not have.

A3 · Letter

bearing

The same seated ball, with a stem: a lowercase b. It can only be this name, which makes it the most ownable. It is also the busiest of the four.

A4 · Two races

bearing

A crossed with the two-ring logo you called not too bad: the ball seated between an inner and an outer ring. The most literal bearing, and the weakest at 16 pixels.

Round bowls and round dots, so the letters belong to the same family as the dots on the page. Sturdy and compact, with numbers that line up in columns and a 1 that cannot be read as a letter.

Things to try

  • Watch a round land. The dot pops in, the open dot moves on, the score ticks and the tint shifts towards whoever is ahead.
  • Click a team's name in the wide header to give it the round yourself.
  • Tap the live card on the phone to open the match, and the arrow to go back.
  • Tap the four icons. The marker slides; each screen is real: pick a day, unfollow a team, type in the search.
  • Let a map finish. The line of stops moves on to the next map. When the series ends, the card says so, then the next match steps forward.
  • Hover a dot to see which round it was.

A · A draw is sharedMY PICK

A dot is a game; a drawn game is half one player's and half the other's. One row, and the score can be counted from it.

B · A draw is small

Only decisive games get a full dot; draws shrink to a grey point. Wins stand out, but the points are harder to add up.

Each player has a row: a full dot for a win, a small one for a draw, a space for a loss. Clearest for the score, twice the height.

One row per driver, a dot for each round won. The standings are visible at a glance, which is what a final is about.

My pick is C. With four or more competitors, the order they stand in matters more than the order the rounds fell.

The rule I'd draw from this: on a card, a dot is whatever the headline score counts: rounds in a Counter-Strike map, games in a League, Rocket League or chess match, rounds won by a driver. The finer picture, such as goals or objectives on the clock, belongs on the match page.

5How the logo is drawn Last time this drawing was wrong: the mark sat about 12% too large against its own guide lines. Here it is in three steps.

1 · A ring

The small bar marks how thick it is. Everything else is measured against that.

2 · A ball on it

One and a half times as wide as the ring is thick. Its centre sits in the middle of the ring's band, up and to the right at 45°.

3 · Cut the ring back

The ring is trimmed around the ball, leaving an even gap a third of the ring's thickness. That is the finished mark.

What changes: nothing in the logo itself; the logo files in the repo were always correct. The mark is one colour. I have dropped the version with a coloured ball, since no colour should read as the brand's.
